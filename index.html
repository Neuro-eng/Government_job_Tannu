Documents to keep ready

10th certificate (date of birth) · Aadhaar or photo ID · B.Tech degree and all semester marksheets with a percentage certificate · experience letters and last salary slips · passport photo and signature scans · category certificate if you claim one (OBC non-creamy layer on or after 1 Apr 2026, EWS for FY 2025-26) · No Objection Certificate if you work in government or a PSU · working email, mobile number and online payment method.

Facilities in central government jobs

LTC: one hometown and one all-India journey per 4-year block (2026–2029) for you and family, after one year of service. Railways: privilege passes. Public sector banks: usually leave fare concession every 2 years. PSUs and ports: staff guest houses and their own travel schemes. Circuit houses and rest houses are mainly for official tours and allotted by grade.
